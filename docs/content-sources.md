## Editorial conventions

- British English is used in prose. Official article and project titles retain the spelling of their source records.
- MD, DDD, PPB, GND, BCC, and W-Re are expanded or explained in their relevant page context.
- The nickel PPB framework is described as two-dimensional; the tungsten network workflow is three-dimensional and uses OpenDiS.
- Current simulated mechanical tests are distinguished from the earlier SHPB experiments. Research aims are distinguished from completed outcomes.
- Each publication preserves the author's name as shown in that record; these name variants do not alter the consistent professional name used for the website.

The supplied CVN is an author-provided source. Appointment and experimental records have not been independently authenticated against employment letters or laboratory records. TMS presentation-title wording is CVN-sourced; no full abstract record was recovered. The IMPLAST booklet was available through the indexed organiser-hosted record, although direct retrieval timed out. The existing public CV is unchanged and still contains older appointment dates/titles and a preprint under its manuscripts-in-preparation heading; these cross-document discrepancies should be resolved before publication if the downloadable CV is to be fully consistent with the webpage.
