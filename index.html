Research profile

Mechanistic materials modeling across length scales.

I develop physics-based computational approaches that connect defect-scale mechanisms with the measurable response of structural materials.

My research combines molecular dynamics, three-dimensional discrete dislocation dynamics, microstructure generation, and scientific data analysis. The central goal is to explain how dislocations interact with cracks, precipitates, grain boundaries, and prior particle boundaries, and how those interactions control plasticity, strengthening, and fracture.

I earned my Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering from IIT Bombay. Most recently, I served as a Visiting Researcher in the Department of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering at Merrimack College, where I investigated crack-tip plasticity and ductile-to-brittle transition behavior in tungsten and tungsten-rhenium systems.
